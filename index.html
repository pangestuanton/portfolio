Aviona Sync details

How Aviona Sync works

Aviona Sync retrieves assignment data, stores it in a structured format, and sends schedules and reminders through services students already use.

Authentication

Google OAuth 2.0 handles authentication and token management.

Synchronization

Scheduled jobs retrieve updates from Classroom and Calendar.

Notification

The Telegram Bot sends automated summaries and reminders.

Storage

MySQL stores users, assignments, statuses, and schedules.
